∫ Continuous Time

Basic Signals, Systems, Convolution, Fourier & Laplace

5 simulations · $\Omega, F$
SIM 01

∿ Basic Signals

The fundamental building blocks — the unit impulse $\delta(t)$, the unit step $u(t)$, the exponential $e^{at}$, and the sinusoidal $\cos(\Omega t)$ — are examined. Through the interactive signal library, parameter variations may be observed in real time.

InteractiveOpen ⚡
 SIM 02

T{·} LTI System Properties

Linearity, time-invariance, causality, stability, and memory properties are considered within the LTI framework. A user-defined $T\{x(t)\}$ expression may be directly tested against five fundamental system properties.

InteractiveOpen ⚡
 SIM 03

∫ Continuous-Time Convolution

The continuous-time LTI output is computed through $y(t)=\int x(\tau)h(t-\tau)\,d\tau$. Sliding, overlap region, and the resulting output curve may be examined interactively, step by step.

InteractiveOpen ⚡
 SIM 04

𝓕 Fourier Series

The decomposition of periodic signals into harmonic components $\Omega_0, 2\Omega_0, \ldots$ is examined. The reconstruction of square, triangular, and sawtooth waveforms through the Fourier series expansion is demonstrated.

InteractiveOpen ⚡
 SIM 05

ℒ Laplace Transform

The Laplace transform $X(s)=\int x(t)\,e^{-st}\,dt$ is visualised as a three-dimensional surface over the $s$-plane. The pole–zero distribution and the Fourier relationship along the $j\Omega$ axis may be jointly examined.

3DOpen ⚡
Σ Discrete Time

Discrete Convolution, Sampling, DTFT, DFT/FFT & Z-Plane

5 simulations · $\omega, f$
SIM 06

Σ Discrete-Time Convolution

The discrete-time LTI output is computed via $y[n]=\sum_k x[k]\,h[n-k]$. The overlap region and the product–sum operation may be traced step by step using the slider.

InteractiveOpen ⚡
 SIM 07

↯ Sampling and Aliasing

The sampling relation $x_s(t)=x(t)\cdot p(t)$ is examined: multiplication by a Dirac comb in the time domain corresponds to convolution with the same comb in the frequency domain, producing a spectrum that is periodically replicated at $F_s$. When the Nyquist condition is violated, aliasing is highlighted interactively.

Draw+PresetOpen ⚡
 SIM 08

𝓕* Discrete-Time Fourier Transform (DTFT)

The continuous and $2\pi$-periodic structure of the DTFT $X(e^{j\omega})=\sum_n x[n]\,e^{-j\omega n}$ is visualised along the $\omega$ axis. The unit circle together with the magnitude and phase panels may be examined jointly through a synchronised cursor.

PeriodicityOpen ⚡
 SIM 09

⏃ Discrete Fourier Transform (DFT/FFT)

The DTFT sampled at $\omega_k=2\pi k/N$ is computed through $X[k]=\sum_n x[n]\,e^{-j2\pi kn/N}$. The effects of the window function, zero-padding and spectral leakage, as well as the performance comparison between $\mathcal{O}(N^2)$ and $\mathcal{O}(N\log N)$ complexities, may be examined.

BenchmarkOpen ⚡
 SIM 10

𝒵 Z-Transform

The Z-transform $X(z)=\sum_n x[n]\,z^{-n}$ is presented as a three-dimensional surface over the $z$-plane. The region of convergence (ROC), the pole–zero placement, and the relationship to the DTFT along the unit circle are jointly considered.

3DOpen ⚡
★ Other

Complementary Application Examples

6 simulations · modulation, STFT, compression, EMD, Doppler, FrFT
SIM 11

Radio — Modulation

Multiple audio channels are modulated onto distinct carrier frequencies; at the receiver, the target channel is recovered by means of a local oscillator and a low-pass filter (LPF). The fundamental operating principles of AM, FM, QAM, and OFDM are addressed within this framework.

TunerOpen ⚡
 SIM 12

2D Spectrum

A two-dimensional spectrum stream (waterfall) based on the Short-Time Fourier Transform (STFT) is provided. Eight different window functions (Hann, Hamming, Blackman, Nuttall, Flat-top, etc.) and an overlap ratio between 0% and 90% may be configured parametrically.

WindowOpen ⚡
 SIM 13

𝒟 Discrete Cosine Transform (DCT) — Compression

Signal compression by means of the Discrete Cosine Transform (DCT) is examined. When the dominant coefficients are retained and the low-energy coefficients are discarded, the proximity of the reconstructed signal to the original is measured. The DCT constitutes the fundamental transform underlying JPEG compression.

InteractiveOpen ⚡
 SIM 14

ℋ Hilbert-Huang Transform (HHT)

A signal is decomposed into Intrinsic Mode Functions (IMFs) by means of Empirical Mode Decomposition (EMD); the Hilbert transform is then applied to obtain the instantaneous frequency and energy density. The method is employed in the analysis of nonlinear and non-stationary signals.

EMDOpen ⚡
 SIM 15

Doppler — Velocity Detection

A moving target is steered in a top-down scene; a radar pulse train is transmitted and the echo is received, and the Doppler shift $f_d$ in the echo is measured. The SNR (−15…+9 dB, and ∞), clutter, the MTI filter, the FFT spectrum, and the time–velocity waterfall may be examined interactively.

RadarOpen ⚡
 SIM 16

𝓕α Fractional Fourier Transform (FrFT)

The thin-lens optical analogy is taken as the basis: a user-drawn pattern is successively transformed through the input, intermediate, and focal planes via Fractional Fourier Transforms (FrFT), parameterised by $\alpha=z/f$. A one-dimensional signal library, chirp generation, freehand drawing, and audio upload are supported; the $\alpha$ slider enables the observation of the $\mathrm{Re}/\mathrm{Im}/|\cdot|$ components and the corresponding rotation in the Cartesian $t$–$\Omega$ plane.

2D+1DOpen ⚡
