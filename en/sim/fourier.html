Fourier Series

A periodic signal of period $T_0$ is expressed by means of the complex Fourier series as $$x(t) = \sum_{k=-\infty}^{+\infty} c_k\,e^{jk\Omega_0 t}, \qquad \Omega_0 = \frac{2\pi}{T_0}$$ and the coefficients are obtained from $$c_k = \frac{1}{T_0}\int_{T_0} x(t)\,e^{-jk\Omega_0 t}\,dt.$$ The two-sided magnitude spectrum $|c_k|$ is displayed as a stem plot for $k=-K,\ldots,+K$. Since real-valued signals satisfy $c_{-k} = c_k^{*}$, the identity $|c_{-k}| = |c_k|$ holds and the spectrum exhibits even symmetry.

Partial sum: $$x_N(t) = \sum_{k=-N}^{+N} c_k\,e^{jk\Omega_0 t}$$ As $N$ increases, the convergence $x_N(t) \to x(t)$ is attained. At discontinuity points of signals such as the square wave, the Gibbs phenomenon is observed: the overshoot of approximately 9% does not vanish but is progressively confined to a narrower interval.

Application Areas

  • Music recognition: in algorithms of the Shazam type, song fingerprints are obtained from spectral peaks.
  • Medical imaging: in MRI, CT and ultrasound modalities, image formation is performed in the frequency domain (k-space).
  • Image and video compression: in the JPEG, MPEG and HEIC standards, energy compaction is achieved via the DCT (a close relative of the Fourier transform).
  • Radar and satellite systems: Doppler-shift computation, terrain mapping and range estimation in FMCW radars are carried out within this framework.
  • Telecommunications: OFDM (4G/5G/WiFi) constitutes a Fourier-based scheme of transmission on parallel carrier frequencies.
  • Spectrum analysers: signal-quality measurement and EMI/EMC testing are conducted via spectral analysis.
  • Astronomy: spectral analysis of distant celestial bodies serves as the primary tool for determining stellar composition.
  • Seismology: spectral analysis of seismic waves is employed in the estimation of source and epicentre locations.
  • Speech synthesis and recognition: mel-spectrogram representations are used as inputs to deep-learning models.
